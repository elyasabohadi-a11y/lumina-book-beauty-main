# Lumina Booking

Create a modern, high-conversion women's beauty and hair salon website focused purely on booking appointments.

Goal: Fill the calendar.

Buyer state: Ready to act.

Target persona:

Lumina Studio, a premier, upscale women's beauty and hair salon in downtown.

Offers expert hair styling, coloring, advanced skincare, and luxury nail treatments.

Serves busy professionals and modern women who want seamless, high-end styling and efficient self-care sessions.

Tone: Sophisticated, welcoming, practical, elegant. No hype language.

PAGE STRUCTURE (single-page):

1) Hero Section

- Outcome-driven headline focused on elegance, precision, and effortless beauty

- Subtle mention of full-service styling and luxury treatments

- One short supporting line

- Primary CTA button: "Book Your Appointment"

- Secondary text link: "View Services Menu"

2) Services / Treatments Menu

- Visually dominant grid-style layout

- Each service shows:

  - Service name

  - Focus (Hair / Skincare / Nails / Makeup)

  - Duration

  - Starting price / quick info

  - Clear booking action per service

3) Trust & Stylists

- Lead stylist/salon expert profile with portrait

- 3 short credibility bullets (years of experience, premium product standards, precision technique)

- Salon interior imagery (minimal, clean, luxurious, relaxing)

4) Social Proof

- Short, realistic client testimonials (1-2 lines each)

- First name, service received, client type

- High-end gallery of salon results (hair transformations, nail art, skincare glows)

5) Practical Logistics

- Base location: Downtown, City Center

- Operating hours & booking policies

- Session length / what to expect

- Who it's for / not for (very brief, practical)

6) Repeat Booking CTA

- Large, clean CTA section prompting instant appointment scheduling

STYLE & DESIGN:

- Modern, minimal, performance-driven aesthetic

- Soft luxury base colors (cream, beige, soft dark or neutral tones)

- ROSE GOLD or WARM CHAMPAGNE as the primary accent color (used sparingly for CTAs, highlights, availability)

- Strong typography hierarchy

- Clean spacing and grid layout

- Schedule/Booking section should feel like a functional system, not marketing content

EXCLUSIONS (do not include):

- Long paragraphs or heavy text walls

- No lead forms

- No blog content

- No email capture forms

- No generic motivational copy

Mobile-first.

Fast-loading.
